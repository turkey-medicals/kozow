Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Canthoplasty?

Canthoplasty is a surgical procedure that reshapes the outer or inner corner of the eyes, improving eye symmetry and lifting drooping eyelids for a rejuvenated appearance.

2. How long is the recovery period?

Most patients recover within 7–14 days for minor swelling and bruising. Complete healing and final results appear after 1–2 months.

3. Will the results look natural?

Yes. Our surgeons specialize in subtle, natural-looking canthoplasty outcomes tailored to each patient’s eye shape and face.

4. Is Canthoplasty safe?

Yes, when performed by certified, experienced surgeons in accredited clinics. Pre-surgery consultation ensures suitability and risk assessment.

5. Can Canthoplasty be combined with other procedures?

Absolutely. Many patients combine canthoplasty with blepharoplasty, brow lift, or non-surgical eye rejuvenation for optimal results.
